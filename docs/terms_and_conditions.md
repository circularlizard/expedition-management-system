## 3. Account Registration, Roles, and Access Security

**3.1. Authentication and Integration:**
Access to the EMS is integrated with Online Scout Manager (OSM) via secure OpenID Connect (OIDC) authentication. 

**3.2. Role-Based Access Guards:**
To protect youth member data and safety:
* Access to register candidates or view signup details is strictly restricted to verified **Parents/Carers** and **Network Members** (alongside authorized Site Administrators).
* Non-authorized roles (including standard Explorer accounts or unauthorized local leaders) are not permitted to proceed past the login gate. 
* The system actively runs automated session guards. If you log in with an unauthorized role, the system will automatically log you out and restrict access to the form page.

**3.3. Account Security Obligations:**
It remains the user’s responsibility:
* To keep your login credentials and passwords secure.
* To protect your devices from unauthorized access while logged into the EMS.
* To clear browser caches and session storage when logging out, especially on shared or public computers (the EMS is designed to assist by clearing temporary `sessionStorage` values automatically on successful form submissions and logouts).

## 4. Route Submissions, GPX Files, and Expedition Safety

**4.1. Navigation and Route Planning:**
Teams and leaders may upload GPX files and route plans into the EMS to coordinate trail safety, check checkpoints, and obtain assessor feedback. 

**4.2. Safety Disclaimer:**
* All route coordinates, GPX files, and map references uploaded to the EMS are tools to support expedition planning and coordination. They **do not replace** the requirement for physical map and compass navigation skills, proper field training, and active leader supervision.
* SESS and its assessors review submitted routes for general planning compliance. However, SESS excludes all liability for any errors, terrain changes, path closures, or navigation difficulties encountered in the field. Real-time safety decisions remain the sole responsibility of the expedition team and their designated supervisors.

## 5. Personal Data Handling (For Leaders and Volunteers)

**5.1. Data Controller and Processor Compliance:**
Anyone accessing personal data (including participant lists, team contacts, emergency numbers, or medical information) via the EMS must handle such data in strict compliance with current Data Protection legislation (UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018).

**5.2. Personal Data Mandates:**
* **LEGITIMATE SCOUTING USE ONLY:** You must only access, download, or use participant information for the specific, legitimate scouting/expedition safety coordination required by your authorized role.
* **SECURE STORAGE:** If you must download medical forms, emergency contacts, or participant lists (e.g., for offline use during a wilderness trip), you must ensure they are kept secure from loss, theft, or unauthorized access by others.
* **IMMEDIATE DELETION:** You must securely delete or destroy all downloaded personal data records as soon as they are no longer required for the safety of the specific expedition.
* **RESTRICTED SHARING:** You must not share participant contact info or personal details with any external third party, nor with other members of Scouting unless they have an authorized role on the same event.
